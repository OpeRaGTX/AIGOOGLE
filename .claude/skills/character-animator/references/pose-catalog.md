# Pose catalog — poses of greatness

Values are offsets for the studio rigs (facing -Y, hips rest height ≈ 1.1 m, `rig.turn` angles in degrees:
pitch + = lean forward, yaw − = turn to the character's right). Scale distances by character height / 1.7 m.
Each pose lists what makes it read; check the silhouette from the camera before keeping it.

## King's stance (стойка короля) — idle with authority
- Feet wider than shoulders, front foot turned out 10–20°, back foot 25°; weight 60% on the back leg.
- Hips: dz −0.04…−0.07 (soft knees), yaw toward the back foot −10°; chest up: torso pitch −4…+4, head pitch −5
  (chin slightly up), head yaw back toward camera (+5…10 vs the chest) — the look comes before the body.
- Weapon planted or resting on the shoulder; free hand open, slightly away from the hip (not glued to the body).
- Life: moving hold — 2–4 cm breathing on hips z over 30–60 frames, hair springs do the rest.

## Overhead wind-up (замах над головой)
- Anticipation first: hips dz −0.25…−0.30 and back +0.06, torso pitch −12…−18 (leaning back), yaw −20 (coil),
  weapon behind and low (shaft angle −135…−150°), head pitch +10…+15 (eyes stay on the target).
- Rise: hips lead (+0.3 m), chest catches up 1–2 frames later and arches (pitch −20…−25 at the apex), weapon passes
  over the head on an arc with 3–5 keys; slow-in at the apex (a 2–3 frame moving hold), then the whip.

## Lunge (выпад)
- Front foot 0.6–0.8 m ahead, knee over the toes; back leg almost straight, heel can lift.
- Hips drop 0.2–0.3 and travel forward faster than the chest (chest arrives +2 f), torso pitch +15…+25.
- The reaching arm extends fully only at the contact frame; the other arm swings back for balance (counter-motion).
- Overshoot: hips go 5 cm past, settle back over 6–10 frames.

## Heavy landing (тяжёлое приземление)
- Contact frame: feet staggered by 1 frame, legs still fairly straight; next 2–3 frames compress hips to −0.35…−0.42,
  torso pitch +40…+48 (keeps falling a frame after the feet), head +30.
- Recoil +0.08…+0.10, second dip −0.05, settle — each bounce about half the previous, total ~15–20 frames.
- One hand/weapon on the floor reads heavier; feet stay planted (no slide), skirt/hair keep falling and bounce.

## Spin with the blade (разворот с мечом)
- Anticipation: counter-turn 20–30° the other way, weapon drawn across the body.
- Spin: hips lead the turn, chest +2 f, weapon +1–2 f behind the hands; 360° in 8–12 frames at 30 fps, fastest in
  the middle; the head spots — stays on the target as long as possible, then whips around ahead of the chest.
- Arcs: keep the blade tip on a circle (top view check); add a slash trail on the frames where it moves fastest.
- End on a strong silhouette (lunge or king's stance) with an overshoot of 10–15° and a settle.

## Victory / hero finish
- Chest up and open (torso pitch −8), weapon raised or planted, chin up, weight on one leg (contrapposto: hips tilt
  toward the straight leg ~5°, shoulders tilt the other way).
